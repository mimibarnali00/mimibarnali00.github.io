Galaxy Formation and Evolution

My current research involves simulating isolated high-redshift (z>10) galaxies using high-resolution radiation-hydrodynamics simulations to resolve the puzzles posed by recent JWST observations, particularly the unexpected overabundance of UV-bright, compact "Blue Monsters." While analytical models like the Attenuation-Free Model (AFM) suggest stellar radiation pressure drives dusty outflows to make galaxies bluer and brighter, they rely on simplified 1D geometries. Cosmological simulations also fail to match these observed UV brightness levels, likely missing key pre-supernova radiation feedback. By incorporating radiation-hydrodynamics in a 3D setup, my work tests how radiation pressure redistributes dust within a realistic, turbulent, and clumpy interstellar medium. For more on the topic, view recent publications.

Inflationary Cosmology (MS Thesis)

As part of my MS thesis project at IISER Kolkata, I worked with Dr. Koushik Dutta (Department of Physical Sciences, IISER Kolkata) and Dr. H.V. Ragavendra (Raman Research Institute) in Inflationary Cosmology.

Under this project, I wrote Python codes from scratch to calculate background evolution and the primordial power spectrum for single-field inflation potentials. I also explored non-Gaussianities arising during inflation that evolved into present-day cosmic structures. For my Independent Study with Prof. Rajesh Kumble Nayak (IISER Kolkata), I examined the properties of primordial gravitational waves.

Pulsar Signals & Millisecond Pulsars

As a recipient of the "National Initiative on Undergraduate Science" (NIUS) scholarship, I conducted research with Dr. Bhaswati Bhattacharyya at the National Centre for Radio Astrophysics (NCRA) on two fully funded projects:

  • Pulsar Detection with PRESTO: Used the PRESTO suite to search for pulsar signals in raw data collected by the Giant Metrewave Radio Telescope (GMRT).
  • Millisecond Pulsar Properties: Determined binary periods, orbit eccentricities, and companion mass relations for 200+ millisecond pulsars from the ATNF Pulsar Catalogue.
